About Kaletopia

Kaletopia is an art project for humanity. It is a curated encyclopedia of history and ideas where an entry can be read as text, imagery, video, or an interactive simulation, on any device. Some entries are two paragraphs and a photograph. Some let you turn the dials on a model and watch what happens.

It starts with the subjects its editor cares about and grows from there. It is not for profit and never will be. The writing is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0, the same license Wikipedia uses, and the code is MIT. Both live in the open on GitHub, where anyone may propose an entry or improve one.

Why

A good explanation of how something happened is rarer than a record that it happened. Simulations let a reader ask "what if" of history and of ideas, and that is a kind of understanding a paragraph cannot give. Kaletopia is a place to collect those explanations and keep them working.

How it is made

Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No framework, no build system to install. Every entry is a file you can read in a text editor, and a small script stitches the front page together from them.

Contributing

Read the contributing guide and open a pull request. Entries are edited, and the bar is craft, not coverage.

Editor
