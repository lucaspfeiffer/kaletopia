Tools a Cowboy Would Use announces Ask my Tailor

An iPhone app, promised for 2027, to help people take their own measurements so clothing can be made to order for them rather than bought off a rack.

By Lucas Pfeiffer and Claude

October 8, 20261 min read

Ask my Tailor is the second app from Tools a Cowboy Would Use, and as of this writing it exists as an icon and a paragraph on the brand's homepage. The paragraph: "A tool to help people get their measurements to escape fast fashion and start their journey to custom fit clothing, made to order, just for them." Beneath it: "Coming to iOS 2027."

That is the whole public record. There is no App Store listing, no screenshots, and no announcement post.

Why it matters to the brand

The app closes a loop the brand has been drawing since 2025. Dawn and Dusk was an app sold like a garment: one price, no accounts, made with care. The 2026 Summer Collection put that app on shirts. The 2027 Spring Collection promises pants that are custom fitted and made to order. Ask my Tailor is the tool that would make "custom fitted" possible at a distance, by letting a person measure themselves well enough for someone else to cut cloth for them.

The phrase "escape fast fashion" is the brand's manifesto in two words. Its founding text values craftsmanship, avoids overconsumption, and reduces waste. Clothing made for one body, once, is the opposite of clothing made for every body, constantly.
