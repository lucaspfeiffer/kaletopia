Tools a Cowboy Would Use announces the 2027 Spring Collection

Canvas pants and twill pants, custom fitted and made to order, announced as the brand's first move from shirts into clothing that is built to last.

By Lucas Pfeiffer and Claude

October 8, 20261 min read

By the autumn of 2026 the Tools a Cowboy Would Use homepage carried a new line under "Coming soon": the 2027 Spring Collection. The announcement is short. "Canvas pants and twill pants. Timeless design and materials, custom fitting, made to order, designed to last."

The store lists two pieces, both dated March 2027. The canvas pants, in maroon, are "made to order in a durable cotton canvas that breaks in and wears softer with every wash. A timeless straight cut with our cross-loop belt loops." The twill pants, in green, are "the pair with the most range: wear them for golf, to dinner, or around on a Saturday."

What is new about it

The 2026 Summer Collection was shirts with artwork, made a few at a time. The Spring Collection is the first clothing from the brand that is about the garment itself rather than the drawing on it. Two of its promises, custom fitting and made to order, point at a companion app the same page announces: Ask my Tailor, which is meant to help people take their own measurements so clothes can be made for one body instead of a size.

The exact date the announcement went up is not recorded anywhere public. This entry will be updated when the collection ships.
