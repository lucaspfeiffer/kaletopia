Dawn and Dusk gets its 2026 Summer Update

A year of feedback became version 1.1, with a sunrise alarm, streaks, a community gallery, a compass for the sun, and charts that read like a weather flip book.

By Lucas Pfeiffer and Claude

October 8, 20261 min read

Version 1.1.0 of Dawn and Dusk reached the App Store on May 25, 2026. The release notes called it the Summer 2026 Update and listed, in one breath, "daily Sunrise Alarm, Streaks, Community Gallery, Weather Summary, Sun Table, and Compass." The brand's newsletter followed on June 11 with its first and only post, titled "Dawn and Dusk 2026 Summer Update," which said the app "got an upgrade from over a year of feedback and many months of iterations."

What changed

  • Sunrise alarm. An alarm set to the sun rather than the clock, "to create a ritual of waking up with the sun."
  • Streaks. Tap once within an hour of sunrise or sunset to keep a streak alive. The app had always been about showing up for those two moments; now it kept count.
  • Community gallery. Upload a sunrise or sunset to a public feed. Uploads are explicit and stripped of location data before they leave the phone.
  • Enhanced charts. The daily charts became what the newsletter called "a glanceable weather flip book."
  • Weather summary. A plain-language forecast at short, medium, or long length.
  • Sun table. A year of sunrise and sunset times in one place.
  • Compass. "Find my, but for the sun." Point the phone and see where the sun will come up or go down.
  • Offline mode and a more compact tab bar.

Point releases through the summer fixed and polished: 1.1.1 in June, 1.1.2 in August, and 1.1.3 at the end of September with widgets. The terms stayed the same as at launch. "No ads, no data collected, one-time purchase on the iOS store."

The release page ends with a line that reads like the brand's whole reason for the app: "Made for all of us, to get outside more."
