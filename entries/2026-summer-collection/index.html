Tools a Cowboy Would Use launches the 2026 Summer Collection

A limited run of made-to-order shirts, each hand embroidered with hand-drawn artwork and a patch for when it was made, that raised Dawn and Dusk "into a category of legendary tools."

By Lucas Pfeiffer and Claude

October 8, 20261 min read

In May 2026, Tools a Cowboy Would Use opened a store and put clothing in it for the first time. The brand had always called itself a clothing brand that happened to make apps. The 2026 Summer Collection was the first time the description was literally true.

The collection is a set of shirts, each made to order. Its own copy says it best: "Each item is made to order, hand embroidered, hand drawn artwork, with a patch for when it was made. This collection elevates Dawn and Dusk by Tools a Cowboy Would Use into a category of legendary tools."

Legendary tools

The shirts are grouped under that phrase, and each one is a drawing of a tool the brand admires:

  • Dawn and Dusk, the brand's own app, dated January 2025.
  • Mail, with artwork inspired by Apple Mail.
  • Maps, inspired by Apple Maps.
  • Notes, described as "the closest thing we have to human .md files."
  • Plane, inspired by the Wright Brothers.
  • Steel, inspired by the steel bar from RuneScape.

A seventh shirt, Let's Grow, sits outside the set and is credited to a friend of the brand in Nashville. An earlier shirt from June 2025, "Tools a Cowboy Will Use," is listed as "the first shirt, a reminder of the spirit behind the work" and is not for sale.

Only some pieces can be bought; the rest are shown. That is in keeping with a project that calls itself art first. The collection has no press and was never advertised beyond the brand's own site and a small Instagram account. Its purpose was to put the app it loves on a shirt, by hand, a few at a time.
